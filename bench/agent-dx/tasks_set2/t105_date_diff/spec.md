Each input line holds two dates `YYYY-MM-DD YYYY-MM-DD` (years 1900 to 2100, proleptic Gregorian with the usual leap-year rule). Print `DAYS WEEKDAY`, where DAYS is the absolute number of days between the dates and WEEKDAY the English weekday name (`Monday` ... `Sunday`) of the earlier date. A line that is not two valid dates (bad format, month or day out of range for that month/year) prints `invalid`. Blank lines are skipped.
