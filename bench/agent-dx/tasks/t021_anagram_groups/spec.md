The input is whitespace-separated words (any number of lines). Compare words case-insensitively: convert each to lower case first. Two words are anagrams if they contain the same letters with the same multiplicities.

Group the distinct lower-cased words into anagram classes. For every class with at least two distinct words print one line with its words sorted ascending and separated by spaces. Print the lines sorted ascending by their first word. If there are no such classes print `none`.
